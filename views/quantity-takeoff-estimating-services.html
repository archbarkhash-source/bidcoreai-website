How It Works

From Drawing Set to Bid-Ready Number

A fixed quote and a delivery date before any work starts — no hourly meter running while we read your set.

1
Send the drawing set
Upload the plans, schedules, specifications and any addenda — or paste a Dropbox, WeTransfer, Google Drive or OneDrive link if the set is large.
2
Scope analysed, then reviewed by an estimator
The BidcoreAI agent reads every sheet and specification section and returns the full scope by division. An estimator reviews it against the drawings line by line. You get a fixed quote and a delivery date before work begins.
3
RFIs raised before measuring
Anything the drawings leave ambiguous is written up as an RFI rather than assumed, so the quantities are not built on a guess that surfaces during construction.
4
Measured, then priced if you want it priced
Quantities are measured in BidcoreAI. If the engagement includes estimating, they are priced on your cost database with markup and overhead applied.
5
Delivered in your own format
With the marked-up set, the assumptions memo and the exclusions list beside the numbers. Typically 24–72 hours depending on the size of the set.

Questions

Common Questions

?
What is the difference between a quantity takeoff and a cost estimate?
A quantity takeoff is the measurement — how much material and work the drawings call for, with no money attached. A cost estimate applies rates to those quantities and adds markup and overhead to reach a bid number. We provide both, and you can engage either on its own.
?
How much do quantity takeoff services cost?
Quoted per project rather than per hour or per sheet. Send the drawing set and you get a fixed price and a delivery date before any work starts, so the cost is known before you commit.
?
How long does a takeoff take?
Typically 24 to 72 hours, depending on the size and complexity of the set. Larger multi-division packages are scheduled against your bid date when you send the drawings.
?
Which CSI divisions do you cover?
All of them, from Division 02 existing conditions through Division 32 exterior improvements, plus the MEP divisions. Output is organised by current CSI MasterFormat sections and mapped to your own cost codes on request.
?
Can you price on our own cost database?
Yes. Rates are held as master and custom libraries you control, with your own assemblies and cost codes. If you do not have a library yet, we can price on national benchmark pricing instead and build the library as you go.
?
What file formats do you accept and deliver?
Send PDF drawing sets, schedules, specifications and addenda — or a link to them if the set is large. Delivery is in your own format, or Excel, CSV and PDF, with the marked-up set alongside the quantities.
