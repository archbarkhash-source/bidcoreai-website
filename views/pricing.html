Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What do the plans cost?
Pricing depends on how many people are bidding and how many projects you run at once, so we quote it rather than list it. Book a demo and you will have a figure the same day — there is no multi-week procurement dance.
Which plan should I start on?
Starter suits a single estimator who needs takeoff, CSI estimating and proposals. Pro adds the risk analyser, bid leveling and a team. Premium is for firms that need org-wide directories, onboarding and priority support.
Is the Go/No-Go analyzer really free?
Yes, and it is free on every plan including no plan at all. It runs on your own SAM.gov API key, so the results and the rate quota are yours. No card, no trial clock.
Can I move between plans later?
Yes. Your cost database, takeoffs and proposal history stay with your workspace, so changing tier does not mean rebuilding anything.
Do you do the takeoff for us?
We can. Our experienced US Estimators measure your drawings and deliver the quantities straight into your project. It is an add-on on Starter and Pro, and included on Premium.
What happens on a demo?
We run your own solicitation through it — scope extraction, risk flags, takeoff and a draft proposal — so you judge it on your work rather than a sample project.
