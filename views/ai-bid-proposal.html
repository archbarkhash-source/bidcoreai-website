Proposal Generation

A proposal drafted
from your own bid.

Cover letter, technical approach, management plan and price schedule drafted from the bid you just priced. Export as .doc or PDF.

What it does

Structured sections
Cover letter, technical approach, management plan and price schedule as separate, editable sections.
Refine any section
Rewrite a single section without touching the rest. No limit on revisions.
Export and submit
.doc or PDF, formatted to attach to a SAM.gov submission.

How it works

1
Draft from the project
Sections generated from the scope, quantities and costs already in the project.
2
Edit what matters
Refine section by section, with your own boilerplate available.
3
Export
Word or PDF, submission-ready.

Questions

Is the output federal-compliant?
The section structure follows what federal solicitations ask for — RFP, IFB, IDIQ, RFQ and Sources Sought. A person still reviews it against the specific solicitation before submitting.
Can I use our own boilerplate?
Yes. Your proposal boilerplate library is kept in the platform and pulled into drafts.
