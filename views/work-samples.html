What are sample preconstruction deliverables?

Sample preconstruction deliverables are example files showing how estimating, quantity takeoff, trade measurement and documentation output are prepared before construction starts. They let a project team review accuracy, formatting and scope structure without committing a live bid to it. Every sample below comes from real completed work with client names, addresses and pricing removed.

First Project Offer

Your First Takeoff Comes Back as a Sample

Every new customer gets their first takeoff delivered as a sample, measured on their own drawings rather than on ours. You see the format, the traceability and the assumptions memo on a real project of yours before committing to anything.
