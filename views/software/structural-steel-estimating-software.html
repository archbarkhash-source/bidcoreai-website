What it is

Tonnage Is Not the Whole Steel Estimate

Structural steel is priced two ways at once. The material is bought by weight, so tonnage matters. The fabrication and erection are driven by piece count and connection complexity, so a light frame made of many small members can cost more to build than a heavier one made of fewer long spans. An estimate that reports only tons has priced the mill order and guessed at the shop.

BidcoreAI measures the framing plans member by member: section designation, length, level and grid location. Weight per foot converts those lengths to tonnage by section, so the estimate can be checked against a mill quote. At the same time the piece count is retained, and connections are counted from the typical details and the connection schedule — shear tabs, moment connections, base plates, stiffeners, bracing gussets — because they are what fills a shop.

The rest of Division 05 is measured on its own terms rather than folded into a tons figure. Metal deck by type, gauge and area with side lap and end lap allowances. Open-web joists and girders by mark, span and depth from the joist schedule. Stairs, rails, ladders, embeds, bollards and lintels as a miscellaneous metals package, which on a fit-out or a parking structure is often larger than the frame itself.

Finishes and treatment are separated as well. Shop primer, galvanising, intumescent and spray-applied fireproofing each carry their own cost per square foot of surface area, which does not track tonnage — a job full of small members has far more surface area per ton than one made of heavy sections, and pricing fireproofing by weight is how that difference gets missed.

Scope

What the Software Measures on a Steel Package

Framing plans, sections, connection details, joist and deck schedules, all read together.

Structural Framing

Beams, columns and bracing measured by section and length across every framing level.

  • Beams and girders by section
  • Columns by section and lift
  • Bracing, struts and kickers
  • Length, level and grid retained
Tonnage Conversion

Weight per foot applied by section designation to give tonnage a fabricator can quote against.

  • Tons by section designation
  • Grade and specification separated
  • Piece count retained alongside weight
  • Mill order summary by shape
Connections

Counted from the typical details and the connection schedule, because they drive shop hours.

  • Shear and moment connections
  • Base plates and anchor rods
  • Stiffeners and doubler plates
  • Bracing gussets and splices
Metal Deck

Decking by type, gauge and profile with lap allowances rather than a raw plan area.

  • Roof and floor deck by type
  • Gauge, depth and finish
  • Side lap and end lap allowance
  • Closures, pour stops and edge angle
Joists and Girders

Open-web joists taken from the joist schedule by mark, span and depth.

  • Joists by mark and span
  • Joist girders and headers
  • Bridging and bracing
  • Special loading conditions
Miscellaneous Metals

The Division 05 items that make up a whole package on fit-outs and parking structures.

  • Stairs, landings and rails
  • Ladders, grating and trench covers
  • Embeds, angles and lintels
  • Bollards and equipment supports

Benefits

What Changes on a Steel Bid

Chiefly, that the shop is priced from what the shop actually does.

Piece Count Kept

Fabrication hours track pieces and connections, not weight. Keeping both numbers is what stops a light, fussy frame being priced like a heavy simple one.

Tonnage a Mill Can Quote

Summaries by shape, grade and length go straight to a service centre for pricing, instead of being reverse-engineered from a total.

Coatings Priced by Area

Fireproofing and galvanising follow surface area, which diverges sharply from tonnage on light framing. Calculating it properly removes a routine underbid.

Misc Metals Not Lost

Stairs, rails and embeds tracked as their own package rather than a lump allowance, which on a fit-out is often the larger half of the scope.

Deck With Laps

Lap allowances applied and visible means the deck order matches the deck installed, rather than arriving 6% short.

Design Changes Tracked

A re-issued framing plan is compared against the last, so a section size change is a flagged difference instead of an expensive surprise.
