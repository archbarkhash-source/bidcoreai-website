AI in Construction

Where AI actually helps in preconstruction

Reading, extracting and comparing — the parts of preconstruction that are volume work. Not judgement, and not pricing.

13 June 20266 min read

AI in construction gets discussed either as inevitable or as hype. It is more useful to be specific: some parts of preconstruction are volume work with clear inputs, and those are where AI bidding software earns its place. The rest is judgement, and it does not.

Where it genuinely helps

Reading long documents

A solicitation package can run to hundreds of pages across specifications, statements of work and addenda. Extracting scope and requirements from that is exactly where consistency beats a person on page 240 at 6pm.

Finding what changed

Comparing Rev 01 to Rev 02, or an addendum to the base set, is mechanical and easy to get wrong by hand. It is also where expensive misses come from.

Surfacing risk for review

Flagging uncapped liquidated damages, unpriced allowances or a missing schedule is pattern matching against known problems. The decision stays with a person — but they get a list instead of a hunt.

Qualifying opportunities at volume

Scoring many opportunities against fixed criteria is arithmetic. Doing it consistently every time is the part people are bad at.

First drafts

A structured draft from material already in the project is faster to edit than a blank page. The editing is where value is added; the blank page was never the valuable part.

Where it does not

  • Pricing judgement. Your rates, productivity assumptions and read on a market are your competitive position, not a model's.
  • Client and market decisions. Whether an agency is worth pursuing is a business call.
  • Anything unverifiable. If output cannot be traced to a source clause or a checkable number, it cannot be relied on in a bid.

The test worth applying

Ask where the output comes from. A tool that cites the clause, page or criterion gives you something checkable. One that produces a confident summary with no provenance asks you to take a bid decision on faith.

The useful framing is not automation. It is that a person should spend their time deciding rather than searching.
